The Content Setup

Make Claude plan videos that sound like you.

Ten minutes. Copy, paste, fill four blanks.

01 · The problem

He asked it what everyone asks.

What Marco typed
Give me video ideas for my food truck.
What he got
1. Behind the scenes
2. Customer reviews
3. Daily specials

Nothing is wrong with the AI.

It doesn’t know who you are, who stops at your window, or how you talk. So it gives you the ideas it gives everyone. Everyone posts them. Nobody watches.

Tell it once. It stops guessing.

02 · The setup

Three moves. Ten minutes.

1

Make a project.

Open Claude, go to Projects, create a new one and name it after your business.

2

Paste the block.

Copy the text below into the project’s instructions.

3

Fill four blanks.

Once. After that, every chat in this project already knows you.

No project feature on your plan? Paste the block as the first message of a new chat instead.

03 · Copy this

Paste this into the project.

You help me plan short videos for my business. Read this before you suggest anything.

WHO I AM
[Your name, what you do, where. One true detail only you have.]

WHO WATCHES
[One real person who should see my videos. Where they are and what is on their mind when they scroll.]

HOW I TALK
[Three things I would say out loud. Three things I would never say.]

WHAT I CAN FILM
[What I see every day that nobody else sees. What I will never film.]

RULES
1. Ideas come from my real day, not from trends I would have to fake.
2. Start every idea with the first 2 seconds: what we see and the first line.
3. Write the lines the way I talk. Short. No slogans.
4. Never invent numbers, reviews or results. If a fact is missing, ask me one question.
5. Give me five ideas. For each: the moment, the first line, why someone stops.

Then replace the four lines in brackets with your own answers.

04 · An example

Marco’s four blanks.

Who I am

Marco. One food truck, one corner, every burger made when you order it. My grandmother taught me the sauce.

Who watches

People who work around the corner. Lunch break, hungry, ten minutes to scroll before they go back.

How I talk

I say “grab one before it’s gone” and “you’ll be back”. I never say “gourmet”, “artisanal” or “elevated”.

What I can film

The grill a minute before the line. The first bite. The regulars I know by name.

Notice what is missing: no adjectives about himself. Only facts and the way he speaks.

05 · The result

Same AI. New ideas.

Before

Behind the scenes. Customer reviews. Daily specials.

After · one idea
The moment: the empty grill at 11:59, one minute before the line.
First line: “One minute until this corner gets loud.”
After · another
The moment: a regular walks up and says nothing.
First line: “He hasn’t told me his order in a year.”

Marco is a character from our story. The ideas are examples of what the setup produces.

06 · Use it

Now ask for this week’s videos.

The project already knows you. So the request can be short. This one works for Reels, TikTok and Shorts:

Give me 5 video ideas for this week.
This week I have: [what is new, what is happening]

For each one:
1. What we see in the first 2 seconds.
2. The first line on screen, under 8 words.
3. What I say to camera, in my words.

Then pick the one I should post first and tell me why.

Two habits that keep it sharp

When an idea feels fake, say why. “I would never do that on camera.” It adjusts.

When a video works, paste it into the project as an example of your voice.
