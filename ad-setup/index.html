The Ad Setup

Make Claude write ads that sound like you.

Ten minutes. Copy, paste, fill four blanks.

01 · The problem

She asked it like a stranger.

What Rosa typed
Write an ad for my shop.
What she got
Quality tailoring at affordable prices! Visit us today!

Nothing is wrong with the AI.

It doesn’t know who you are, who buys from you, or how you talk. So it writes for everybody, and an ad for everybody is an ad for nobody.

Tell it once. It stops guessing.

02 · The setup

Three moves. Ten minutes.

1

Make a project.

Open Claude, go to Projects, create a new one and name it after your business.

2

Paste the block.

Copy the text below into the project’s instructions.

3

Fill four blanks.

Once. After that, every chat in this project already knows you.

No project feature on your plan? Paste the block as the first message of a new chat instead.

03 · Copy this

Paste this into the project.

You write ads for my business. Read this before you write anything.

WHO I AM
[Your name, what you do, for how long, where. One true detail only you have.]

WHO BUYS FROM ME
[One real customer. What is on their mind the day they find me.]

HOW I TALK
[Three things I would say out loud. Three things I would never say.]

WHAT I WANT THEM TO DO
[One action: come in, call, message, book.]

RULES
1. Write the way I talk. Short sentences. No slogans.
2. Never use "quality", "affordable", "best in town" or exclamation marks.
3. Start with the customer's moment, not with me.
4. If a fact is missing, ask me one question. Never invent prices, numbers or reviews.
5. Give me three versions: about 12, 25 and 40 words.

Then replace the four lines in brackets with your own answers.

04 · An example

Rosa’s four blanks.

Who I am

Rosa. I have made and mended clothes on this street for forty years. I still sew the linings by hand.

Who buys from me

Someone holding a jacket they love. It tore, or it stopped fitting, and they think it is finished.

How I talk

I say “bring it in, let me look” and “it can be saved”. I never say “premium”, “solutions” or “unbeatable”.

What I want them to do

Walk in with the piece they can’t throw away.

Notice what is missing: no adjectives about herself. Only facts and the way she speaks.

05 · The result

Same AI. Different ad.

Before

Quality tailoring at affordable prices! Visit us today!

After · the short one
Bring me the jacket you can’t throw away.
Rosa. 40 years on this street.
After · the longer one
The jacket at the back of your closet. The one you can’t wear and can’t give away. Bring it in. Let me look.

Rosa is a character from our story. The ads are examples of what the setup produces.

06 · Use it

Now ask for this week’s ad.

The project already knows you. So the request can be short. This one is built for Facebook and Instagram ads:

Write a Facebook and Instagram ad for:
[what I am offering this week]

Give me:
1. The first line people see, under 8 words.
2. The main text, under 40 words.
3. A headline, under 6 words.

Then the first 2 seconds of a Reel for it: what we see, and the one line on screen.

Two habits that keep it sharp

When an ad sounds wrong, say why. “Too salesy. I would never say this.” It adjusts.

When one works, paste it into the project as an example of your voice.

One setup of six

This one writes your ads.

The same idea, tell it once, works for the rest of your day:

  • Think a decision through with you, and argue the other side
  • Stay on one task until it is done
  • Remember your business between chats
  • Write the first message to a new customer
  • Never make up a number
